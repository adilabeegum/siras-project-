About SIRAS

SIRAS (Smart Inventory Restock Alert System) is a web-based inventory and billing system designed for small businesses, cafés, and local stores. It helps manage stock, perform billing, and automatically notify restocking needs via web or SMS. The system supports multi-user access and smart AI suggestions for efficient inventory control.

Core Features

Inventory Tracking

Manage product stock with add, edit, and delete options.

Billing System

Generate printable bills with automatic price calculations.

Restock Alerts

Low stock alerts via web or SMS notifications.

Smart Suggestions

AI-based trends & fast/slow selling analysis.

Sales Reports

Visual graphs and tables of daily/monthly sales.

Chatbot

Ask questions like “How many soaps left?” and more.
